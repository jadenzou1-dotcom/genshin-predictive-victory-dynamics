# Round of 16 — Group A

Snapshot: 2026-10-12, ~12:20 ET (pre-lock). Round ended ~22:00 ET; support rates lock ~18:00 ET.

## Match 1: The Mysterious Howl Crew (L) vs Little Fungi Clan (R)

- Howl: Ladler of Broth (66k HP / 1.9k ATK, Ranged, Single-Target) plus 4 Cicin squads ×3 (3k HP / 2.4k ATK each, Flying, Squad).
- Fungi: 2 Anemo Fungus (11k / 8.3k), 2 Hydro Fungus (11k / 6.8k, immobilize), 2 Winged Cryoshroom (20k / 9.2k, Single-Target). All Flying.
- Fungi's AoE hard-counters the Cicin squads. Howl's only counter edge is its weak leader.
- Model: Howl 8.5%. Crowd: 25%.

## Match 2: Frostarm and Shaman Formation (L) vs Saurians Are the Cutest (R)

- Frostarm: Lawachurl (74k / 11.8k, Melee, AoE, Frost Armor), 2 shooter squads ×2 (9k / 4.7k), Hydro Samachurl (heals), Anemo Samachurl (vortex).
- Saurians: a single boss, Sappho (123k / 19.4k, Ranged, Single-Target, freeze and Iceshatter).
- Counters favour Frostarm (Melee > Ranged, Squad > Single-Target). Raw stats favour Frostarm by about 2× ATK.
- Model: Saurians 11.9%. Crowd: 13%. Breakeven: 7.3%.

## Results
- **Match 1: Little Fungi Clan won** (model favourite, 91.5%). Fungi finished with ~54% team HP; Howl Crew wiped ~35 s in.
- **Match 2: Saurians won** (model underdog, 11.9%; crowd 13%). Sappho finished with ~26% HP; Frostarm wiped at ~43 s, 1–2 s before the clock ran out.

## Replay observations (from `genshin_one.mp4`, 60 fps)
Measured with `replay_hp_tracker.py` (OpenCV, team HP bars sampled 5×/s → `data/replays/R16-A_hp_bars.csv`) plus frame inspection.

**Battle clock**
- Every battle has a 45 s countdown. The timer turns red at 00:05.
- **No HP drain was visible.** Survivors' HP bars were flat for the last 5–13 s of both matches, including after the timer turned red. If a drain exists, it starts after 00:00 or only applies when both sides are still alive at the end. Untested so far.
- The game keeps playing until the clock ends even after one side is wiped.

**Match 1 (Fungi win)**
- Fungi lost ~25% of team HP in the first ~6 s: the Cicin squads fly forward and deal real damage early.
- Then the Fungi AoE dismantled the squads. Commentary: "the Fungi have the enemy completely surrounded… a cauldron of elemental reactions" (Hydro Cicin/Fungus Hydro + Cryoshroom Cryo + Anemo swirl).
- Ladler of Broth stayed at the back (cabbage bombardment) and was the last Howl unit alive.

**Match 2 (Saurians upset)**
- Sappho alternates Hydro and Cryo, so **she freezes enemies by herself**. Commentary: "Combining the powers of Hydro and Cryo to freeze her enemies — that's one of Sappho's signature techniques."
- **She kites melee.** Commentary: "chaining together dashes to keep the enemy chasing after her". For the final ~13 s only the Lawachurl was alive, and it did **zero** damage (Sappho's HP flat at 26%) because it was frozen or chasing.
- Hydro auras were visible over Frostarm units. Some may come from their own Hydro Samachurl's rain, which would make them easier to freeze. Ambiguous, so it's a calibrated parameter (`rain_on`).
- Frostarm's damage came early. Team-HP traces show Sappho went from 100% → ~50% in the first ~20 s while the squads were alive, then slowed down.

**Why the model missed it**
1. v2 had no elemental reactions, so Freeze, Sappho's main defence, wasn't modeled.
2. Melee units couldn't be kited.
3. The HP-drain assumption (starting 30–90 s in) was wrong for a 45 s battle. The fight is decided purely by trading damage.
