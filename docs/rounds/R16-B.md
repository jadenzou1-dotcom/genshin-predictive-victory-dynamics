# Round of 16 — Group B

## Match 1: Elemental Dye Experts (L) vs Mirror Fruity Order (R)
Snapshot 2026-10-13 21:27 ET, 36 min before the round ended, so the support rate had already locked: crowd 21% / 79%, ×105 / ×30.

- **Left:** Cryo, Electro, Pyro Specters (Flying, 20k HP / 7.8k ATK, each immune to its own element) plus Hydro Mimic Finch (Melee AoE, 46k / 4k, Hydro-immune, explodes on death).
- **Right:** 4 Pyro Potioneers (Ranged AoE, 21k / 6.2k, burning zones on ground targets), 2 Handyman squads ×3 (Melee, **cannot target Flying**), 2 Scout squads ×3 (Ranged, Physical).

**Model (v3 + calibration, never looks at support rates): left wins 77%.**
- Why: 6 of right's 13 bodies (Handymen) can only hit the Finch. The Electro Specter's AoE and the Finch's AoE hard-counter the clustered squads. The Pyro Specter is immune to all four Potioneers.
- Sensitivity: squad HP per member → 69%, split across the squad → 90%. **AoE radius is the biggest unknown: 0.5 cells → 49%, 1.0 → 77%, 1.5 → 90%.**
- Breakeven for left at ×105 vs ×30: 17%. Left has the higher EV under every scenario tested.

## Match 2: Unofficial Blazing Beasts Members (L) vs Rude-a-Lot, Hats-a-Not (R)
Model run **before** seeing support rates.
- **Left:** Eremite Sunfrost (Melee AoE Cryo, 55k / 5.5k), Desert Clearwater (Melee ST Hydro, 55k / 5.5k), Scorching Loremaster (Ranged ST Pyro + Scorpion summon, 34k / 5.5k), Galehunter (Ranged AoE Anemo + Glede summon, 34k / 5.5k).
- **Right:** 3× Big Bro Rude-a-Lot (Ranged ST Pyro, 25k / 9.1k, opening suppressive fire) plus Mechanoid Assistant (Melee, **93k HP / 0 ATK**; each skill gives allies +10% ATK and heals 5% max HP).

**Model: left wins 68%** (AoE radius 0.5 → 57%, 1.5 → 76%).
- **58% of simulations reach the 45 s timeout,** because the Mechanoid's 93k HP is hard to burn down. The model then decides by remaining-HP fraction, and that timeout rule hasn't been seen in a replay yet. It's the biggest risk in this prediction.

## Results and replay (`genshin_two.mp4`)

The Pyro Specter, immune to all four Potioneers, survived alone to overtime and died about 2.5 s after 00:00.
